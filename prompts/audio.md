# Natural Human Speech for AI-Generated Scripts

## The Problem
AI-generated scripts have tell-tale patterns that make them sound robotic:
- Em dashes, semicolons, over-structured punctuation
- "Here's the thing", "It's not X, it's Y", "This is why" openings
- Perfect balanced sentences that all land the same length
- Hedging: "actually", "really", "truly", "very"
- Triads (groups of 3 adjectives)
- Lists that sound like bullet points
- Generic words instead of specific moments/numbers
- Starting with context/setup instead of mid-conflict
- "Cool" or "okay" as transition words
- No contractions, too much explanation

## The Fix: How Humans Actually Talk

### Sentence Structure
- Run-on sentences, no period needed
- Short punchy sentences mixed with long rambling ones
- Start sentences with "And" or "But"
- Trail off mid-thought, finish it out loud
- Questions the speaker already knows the answer to
- Incomplete thoughts
- Casual grammar, dropped articles

### Word Choice
- Contractions naturally
- Specific moments, numbers, names (not generic)
- Real emotions: frustration, confusion, relief
- No over-explaining
- No hedging or disclaimers
- No marketing language or hype

### What to Kill
- Em dashes
- "Here's the thing" / "Here's what..."
- "It's not X, it's Y" formula
- "This is why..." openings
- "In conclusion" / "To sum up"
- Words like: dive into, unleash, game-changing, leverage, unlock
- Filler: very, truly, really, actually (when used to hedge)
- Triads of adjectives

### The Accusation Rule
Real people take stances without hedging. If the script sounds like it's trying to be fair to all sides, it's AI. Real humans say "this is wrong" not "some might argue this could potentially be."

### The Specificity Rule
Real humans say "I went on 47 dates" not "I went on many dates." They say "Hinge" not "dating apps." They say "three messages in" not "a few messages in."

## Quick Audit Checklist
Before finalizing any script, check:
- [ ] No em dashes
- [ ] No "Here's the thing" or "It's not X it's Y"
- [ ] Sentences vary in length (some 3 words, some 25)
- [ ] Starts mid-conflict, not with setup
- [ ] Has specific numbers or moments
- [ ] Uses contractions naturally
- [ ] No hedging words: actually, really, truly, very
- [ ] Sounds like someone talking, not writing
- [ ] Run it out loud — does it feel like someone would actually say this?#
