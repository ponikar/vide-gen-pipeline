Gold Fish — Product Story
The Problem Nobody Talks About
You built something great. An app that solves a real problem. A product that deserves to exist.

But nobody knows about it.

You don't have a marketing team. You don't have an audience. You don't have the time or skills to become a content creator on top of building the product itself. Distribution is broken for indie developers. The only people who win at organic social are the ones who can already afford to be there — the ones with followings, with content instincts, with hours to spare filming hooks and chasing trends.

You're not losing on product. You're losing on distribution.

The Vision
Gold Fish is an AI-powered social media agent that acts as your distribution engine.

Think of it as a social media intern that never sleeps, never stops learning, and works for the equity you're building in your product. It generates content, posts it, reads the signals, and adapts. It runs experiments so you don't have to. It finds what resonates and doubles down.

The goal isn't engagement for the sake of vanity metrics. The goal is installs. Real users finding your product through organic social reach — without you spending a dollar on ads.

The Formula
AI + Scheduler + Consistency + Feedback Loop = Distribution


AI generates short-form video content optimized for the platform's algorithm
Scheduler posts automatically, without you lifting a finger
Consistency builds presence over time — the algorithm rewards regularity
Feedback Loop learns what works and refines the next cycle
What Gold Fish Actually Does
Generates content — creates short-form videos (reels, TikToks) using your product's story as fuel
Posts on a schedule — publishes to Instagram/TikTok via API without manual intervention
Pulls insights — collects performance data (views, shares, profile visits, link clicks)
Learns — identifies which hooks, formats, and topics drive the best results
Iterates — refines content strategy based on what's working

The loop never stops. The agent gets smarter over time.

The Conversion Funnel
View → Profile Visit → Bio Link Click → Landing Page → App Install


Every reel is a recruitment funnel. The algorithm surfaces it, a curious viewer watches, they visit your profile, they click the link, they install the app. Gold Fish owns the top and middle of that funnel — everything from hook to click.
The critical bottleneck is the profile visit rate. That's where most content fails. Gold Fish is built to drive curiosity through the door — not just views.

Why Now
The organic social playbook was locked behind creator knowledge and ad budgets. That's over.

AI can now generate, post, and learn at a pace no human social media manager can match. Short-form video algorithms reward consistency and engagement signals — both things an autonomous agent can optimize for. The cost of entry to organic distribution has collapsed.

The indie developer who figures this out first wins. Not by spending more, but by being the first to actually execute.

Who It's For
Indie developers and small teams who:
Have a product worth promoting
Have zero or minimal social media presence
Don't have time to become content creators
Can't justify ad spend before finding product-market fit
The Wedge
Blaze.ai is the closest competitor — $10M ARR, bootstrapped, YC W22. But they're horizontal (any SMB), general content (posts, blogs, email, ads), not optimized for app install conversion.

Gold Fish is narrow by design: app-specific content, short-form video only, optimized for the install. The wedge is vertical depth, not horizontal breadth.

The window is 12–18 months before a horizontal player moves downmarket into this niche.

The End State
You wake up, check your phone, and there's an alert: "New reel did 12K views. 340 profile visits. 28 app installs. Top hook this week: [X]. Pushing variant B now."

Gold Fish is the distribution layer your product was always missing.
